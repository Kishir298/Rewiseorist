# Rewiseorist — Requirements Checklist

## Purpose and scope

This is the single traceability checklist for Rewiseorist, derived from the Criterion A design brief and appendix, the Criterion B design specifications and feedback, the Criterion C build log, the complete flowchart and the build-process flowchart. Requirements are paraphrased and made testable; long source passages are not reproduced.

The implementation paths and routes below are **targets** from the agreed project layout; confirm them against the current tree before claiming completion. Update the mapping if a path changes, but do not delete a requirement without a recorded approved change.

**Status key:** every item starts as `[ ] Not started`. An item may be marked complete only when its verification evidence is linked in the evidence folder. No requirement may be marked complete by assumption.

**Critical measurable gates:** 39/40 contrast sample; all text/background pairs pass; 10/12 visual appeal; 10/12 navigation; 12/12 onboarding; six pages; 240px desktop sidebar; hover at or under 150ms; 44x44 profile; 48x48 help; 360/768/1366/1920px widths; 20MB file, 50 files and 200MB folder limits; 50 saved sessions; 2/7/10-minute inactivity; five-file `.md` grouping; no paywalls and AED 0 compulsory spend; three personalities; local AI default with optional API.

## Governing decisions

- The six-page frontend is one `frontend/index.html`, not six separate HTML files.
- Revision-answer scoring and learner-performance classification use separate rubrics.
- Both the language model and the performance classifier use multi-teacher knowledge distillation, selecting the best two teachers for each task.
- Training examples are expanded to include learners with similar scores but different hint use and independent-answer behaviour.
- **Git-first overrides the earlier Git-last proposal** in Criterion C step 21. The repository, ignore rules and account access are set up before feature work, with reviewed commits at checkpoints and a final release push.
- **Dataset correction:** the language-model dataset is TinyStories (`roneneldan/TinyStories`, licence CDLA-Sharing-1.0), not a public "PyStories" dataset.

## Human-only checks and evidence integrity

These checks cannot be replaced by generated output. Leave them unchecked until a real person has completed them, and store the raw evidence under `evidence/`.

- **H-01 (links B-07):** 12 real students rate visual appeal; report the actual distribution and require at least 10/12 at 4/5 or higher.
- **H-02 (links B-08):** 12 real students attempt navigation; record task success, time and assistance; require at least 10/12.
- **H-03 (links B-09):** 12 real students complete the five onboarding tasks; require 12/12 and preserve the timing/mistake/assistance sheet.
- **H-04 (links A-03, C-23):** student and educator feedback is collected with consent; do not invent quotes or paraphrase unrecorded opinions as data.
- **H-05 (links C-05, C-12, C-28, C-29):** candidate-model responses, rubric scores and teacher selections come from actual local runs; keep prompts, outputs and scoring sheets.
- **H-06 (links C-10, C-11, C-32, C-35):** training loss, held-out metrics, hardware, timings and failure cases are measured, not estimated or copied from examples.
- **H-07 (links B-19, B-20, B-25):** cold start, server shutdown, weight unload and memory release are observed on the target laptop with Task Manager or Activity Monitor; screenshots must show real processes.
- **H-08 (links B-40, B-42):** keyboard-only, screen-reader and print checks are performed on real browsers/devices with versions recorded.

## Assumptions and source gaps

1. **Contrast standard:** Criterion B requires a contrast checker and says all combinations must pass but does not name a WCAG version or ratio. The audit should use WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 normal text, 3:1 large text) as the verification baseline while still reporting the coursework's 39/40 sample rule.
2. **Onboarding time:** the source requires recorded time, mistakes and assistance, with 12/12 passing, but sets no maximum time. Report actual times rather than inventing a threshold.
